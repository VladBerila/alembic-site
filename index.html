Alembic
An alchemist's codex

Pour, sort, and brew.

Alembic is a colour-sorting puzzle where the sorting is the means and the codex is the point. Pour liquid from bottle to bottle until every colour stands alone. Every fifth level fills a chest, and every chest brews a named potion for your shelf — forty in all, each one something you can actually drink.

Three hundred levels, with the hard ones labelled before you play. Vials and retorts that change what a spare bottle is for. A daily Cauldron, the same board for every player in the world. No timers, no lives, no ads between levels. Fully offline.

For iPhone and iPad. In English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
